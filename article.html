Frequently Asked Questions

Is downloading YouTube thumbnails legal?

Yes, for personal and fair-use purposes. Thumbnails of public YouTube videos are publicly accessible assets. However, reusing thumbnails commercially or claiming them as your own may violate copyright. Always respect creator rights and YouTube's Terms of Service.

Can I download thumbnails from private or unlisted videos?

No. getthumb.cc only works with public videos. Private/unlisted videos restrict thumbnail access via YouTube's API.

Why is the WebP option useful?

WebP images are 25–35% smaller than JPEG/PNG at equivalent quality. Ideal for web developers optimizing page speed and Core Web Vitals.

Yes. The interface is fully responsive. Tap to paste, select quality, tap download — works on iOS Safari, Chrome Android, and all modern mobile browsers.
